133-
Kingston, 2d November, 1781.

MRS. JONES respectfully requests of every Person indebted to her, that they will settle with her, otherwise she will be under the disagreeable Necessity of putting their Accounts in Suit, in order to pay off Demands against herself.



133-36
Kingston, 2d November, 1781.

THE Subscriber intending to depart this Island in the next Spring, requests all Persons who stand indebted to him on open Accounts, to settle the same, without further Delay.

JOHN SYMES.



133-36
Kingston, 2d November, 1781.

LOST, about six weeks ago, in Kingston, or betwixt that and Dawkins's Caymanas, on the road to Spanish-Town, an elegant

Silver SNUFF-BOX,

On the Lid of which was a very fine AGATE, and the inside gilt—Whoever will deliver it at the Royal Gazette printing-office, shall receive a Guinea reward.



133-36
Kingston, 1st November, 1781.

TO BE SOLD,

A MOUNTAIN POLINK in the parish of St. Catherine, commonly called and known by the name of HEALTHFUL HILL, adjoining on Mr. JOHN GARDNER MILLWARD, with or without ten or twelve NEGROES, and within seven miles of Spanish-Town:—it commands Old-Harbour, likewise a pleasant prospect of the Pens, &c. about Spanish-Town. There is on the premises a great variety of Fruit Trees, as likewise a great quantity of Coffee, Ground Provisions, and Plantain Walks.

For further particulars, please enquire on the premises of Mr. John Wilkins, or to the Subscriber in Kingston.

WILLIAM BRYANT.



133-
Admiral's Penn, 2d Nov. 1781.

WHEREAS a dark-brown STEER, between two and three years old, having no brand mark, has been grazing here for a short time past; the Owner of the same, on proving his property, may have it, by applying as above.



132-
Kingston, 26th October, 1781.

RAN AWAY some time ago, a negro boy of the Chambo country, named TOM, marked on one of his shoulders C. G. with a diamond between, he is well known in Kingston, and Spanish-Town, having waited on the subscriber upwards of two years. Any person who will secure the above negro, and deliver him to the subscriber, shall receive ONE PISTOLE reward, and all reasonable charges.

C. GOODWILLIE.



133-134
St. Thomas in the East, Nov. 8, 1781.

TO BE SOLD, by Public Auction, at MOUNT STEWART, near Bath, on Tuesday the 13th Instant, all the—

HOUSEHOLD FURNITURE,

With a small quantity of PLATE, CHINA, GLASS and QUEEN'S WARE, and some BOOKS; belonging to the estate of Mr. Dougald Stewart, deceased:—The goods may be view'd any day previous to the sale, which will begin at 9 o'clock in the morning.

N. B. As there are many pressing demands against the above estate, the gentlemen who may become purchasers are requested not to be offended, if no goods are delivered till paid for.

DONALD MACDONALD,
Adm. of D. Stewart, dec.



133-
St. Mary, 29th Sept. 1782.

TO BE LEASED for a Term of Years, to the highest Bidder, the first Saturday after the 28th day of September 1782, at JOHN HENDERSON'S tavern on Port-Maria Bay, a parcel of PARISH LAND, containing Ninety Acres, now in possession of NATHANIEL BAYLEY, Esq.—The said Lease expires the 12th day of October 1782.

By Order of the Vestry.

R. BENNETT, Clk. Vestry.



133-136
29th October, 1781.

NOTICE is hereby given, to any Persons having Demands against the Estate of Mr. JOHN BURKE, late of St. Thomas in the Vale, Tavern-keeper, deceased, to render in a State of the same to his Executor Mr. JOHN WILLIAMS of Spanish-Town, Carpenter; and those who are indebted thereto are requested to discharge the same, so as to enable the said Executor to pay off the most pressing and prior Creditors, without Suit or Trouble to either Party.



133-34
Spanish-Town, October 31, 1781.

THOSE Persons who have any Demands on the Public, for the Hire of Negroes sent from Kingston to the Works at Port-Royal under the Direction of Alexander Leslie, are desired to send their Accounts IMMEDIATELY to the Engineer.



132-136
Kingston, October 26th, 1781.

THE Creditors of Abraham Tavares, jun. are earnestly requested to meet Mr. Bernal, the surviving assignee, at Mr. Allen's tavern, on Thursday morning the 29th of November next, when matters of very serious consideration will be laid before them, relative to the securing part of their very extensive demands against the said Tavares. It is therefore hoped all the creditors will make a point of attending with an exact state of their respective demands.

131-138
October 10th, 1781.

ALL Persons who have any Demands against the Estate of Mr. James Ore, late Copper-smith, deceased, or against the late Copartnership of Ore & Halbert, are requested to render in their Accounts, that they may be settled and paid; and those who are anyways indebted to said Estate or Copartnership, are also requested to be speedy in their Payments to Mrs. Ore at White River in St. George's, who is properly authorised to give Discharges for the same, or to the Subscriber in St. Thomas in the Vale.—As Mrs. Ore proposes leaving the Island early in the Spring, it is hoped that no further Intimation will be required.

The Subscriber likewise begs leave to inform the Public, and his Friends, that he has purchased the Negroes, Stock in Trade, &c. of the said James Ore, and proposes carrying on the Business as usual, both at North and South Side.

JAMES HALBERT.



132-
Iter Boreale, St. George, Oct. 24, 1781.

TAKEN UP on this estate, a stout new-negro man quite naked, has no mark, can speak no English, his country unknown, as no negro can be found that understands him; by cold and bad usage before he was taken up, he got contracted in his limbs, which renders him incapable of walking, otherwise would have sent him to gaol. Whoever owns him may have him by proving his property, and paying for this advertisement, and other incident charges—Apply at the above estate to

HUGH MORISON.

CHARLES WRIGHT.

A complete GARDENER is wanted: please to apply as above.



133-
Kingston, Nov. 3d, 1781.

RAN AWAY, about six weeks since, a negro man named LORRAIN, alias LAWRENCE, about 5 feet 2 inches high, strong and well made; he is marked on the right breast with some letters which are not recollected, and a part of his right ear cut off; he generally wears a handkerchief round his head, is well known in Spanish Town and Kingston, and lately returned from the expedition against Fort St. Juan. It is supposed he will attempt to pass for a free man, as he speaks French and English with considerable fluency—It is requested that all masters of vessels may be careful that he does not prevail on them to take him off the island. A reward of TWO POUNDS will be paid for apprehending him, on applying at the Custom House, to

JOHN BARRY.
